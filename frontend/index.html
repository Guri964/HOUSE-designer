Features

Everything the studio does.

AI-Assisted Residential Planning

Plain-language requirements parsed into bedrooms, area, style, kitchen type and entrance direction.

2D Floorplan Visualization

A dimensioned plan with room labels, doors, orientation and clickable rooms.

Interactive 3D Digital Twin

Orbit, pan and zoom a Three.js model of the generated geometry.

Room & Furniture Planning

Wall-aligned furniture with door clearance, in both plan and twin.

Architectural Validation

Rule checks combined with a GCN layout score — passes, warnings and failures listed plainly.

Cost Estimation

Itemised estimate by category from regional rate profiles, with assumptions and exclusions.

Lighting & Plumbing

Lighting fixtures and Manhattan-routed supply and drainage lines, toggled per layer.

Reports & Export

Printable design report, floorplan PNG and the full design JSON.
